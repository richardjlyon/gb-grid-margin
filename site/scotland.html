When Scotland’s wind stops

Scotland has more than two-fifths of all the wind capacity in Britain (DESNZ, end of 2025). This page follows every large Scottish wind farm on the national grid, about 14 GW in all, from its own settlement meter, and counts the hours when the wind gave almost nothing.
